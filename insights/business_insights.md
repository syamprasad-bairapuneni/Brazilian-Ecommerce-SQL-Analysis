# Key Insights

1. **Late deliveries drive poor reviews.** About 8% of delivered orders (7,701 of 96,359) arrived after the estimated date. These orders averaged a 2.57 review score, against 4.29 for on-time orders.

2. **Repeat customers are rare but high-value.** About 3% of customers ordered more than once (2,913 of 95,420 customers with priced orders). Repeat customers averaged 262.03 in lifetime revenue, against 138.67 for one-time customers.

3. **High-price categories out-earn high-volume ones.** Watches & gifts ranks 7th by items sold (5,991) but 2nd by revenue (1,205,006), at about 201 per item. Bed/bath/table sells more items (11,115) but earns about 93 per item.

## Caveats
- The late-delivery gap is a correlation. Late delivery and low ratings may both reflect other problems, such as seller quality or product issues.
- The repeat-customer revenue gap partly reflects that repeat customers place more orders. Revenue per order is a fairer comparison.
